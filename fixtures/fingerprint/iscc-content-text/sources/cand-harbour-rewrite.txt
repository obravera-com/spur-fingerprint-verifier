Port Meridian's eastern breakwater is to shut for nearly three months from October so that storm-damaged armour units can be replaced, the harbour authority said this week. Ferries, fishing boats and the leisure marina are all affected.

An August survey found about a third of the seaward units had cracked or moved. Chief engineer Marta Keel told a town hall meeting that closing now was the only way to finish before the January gales.

The Salt Island ferry moves to the old western quay with longer crossings; fishing boats go to Coldwater Basin with a crew shuttle laid on. Marina berths cannot be moved, leaving owners to relocate at their own cost, and residents want the council to seek compensation.

The £4.2m scheme is jointly funded by the region and the authority. Work on the northern arm may follow in 2028.
